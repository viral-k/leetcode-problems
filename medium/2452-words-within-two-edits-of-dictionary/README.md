# 2452. Words Within Two Edits of Dictionary

**Difficulty:** Medium  
**Topics:** `Array`, `String`  
**Complexity:** time O(Q * D * N), space O(1)

- **Write-up:** [yral.dev/leetcode/words-within-two-edits-of-dictionary](https://yral.dev/leetcode/words-within-two-edits-of-dictionary)
- **Problem:** [Words Within Two Edits of Dictionary on LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/problems/words-within-two-edits-of-dictionary/)

## Idea

Given small constraints (≤100 for all dimensions), brute force comparison works. For each query, check if any dictionary word differs by at most 2 characters.
